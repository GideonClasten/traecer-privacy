Traecer

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Traecer is a strength-training app for logging workouts and reviewing exercise performance, training history, and progression. It is operated by Gideon Clasten, an individual developer in Norway.

This policy explains what information Traecer processes and the choices available to you. For privacy questions or requests, contact traecer.privacy@gmail.com .

1. Information we process

Account and profile information

We process your email address, account identifier, display name, username, and optional Gender selection and profile photo. Signup also stores your display name, username, and Gender choice in authentication account metadata. Your password is not put in that metadata.

Supabase Auth handles sign-in credentials. Traecer does not save your password in profile or workout records. The app stores authentication session information on your device so you can remain signed in.

Training information

We store the training information you save, including workout names and types, dates and times, exercises, set positions, weights, repetitions, set types, and completion times. This includes workout templates, exercise ordering, custom exercise names and notes, muscle and equipment selections, and custom equipment.

Completed workouts also retain exercise details as they appeared in that workout. Progress, personal records, volume comparisons, and Ghost Sets are calculated from your training records; they are not separate public profiles.

Preferences

We store settings such as language, appearance, weight units, rest-timer duration, previous-weight and repetition preferences, previous-workout visibility, and Spotlight exercise and rotation selections.

When an account is created, settings also store a body-illustration value (female, male, or neutral) based on Gender at that time. Changing Gender later does not update this stored value. Illustrations use your current Gender selection. The stored value is not used for workout or Progress calculations and is not part of a public profile.

Imported training history

When you select a CSV file, Traecer reads it on your device and creates a temporary local cache copy. The current importer does not upload the original CSV file to our backend. Supported Weight + Reps workout information extracted from it is saved as training records.

Exercise and equipment information created during import, and your saved source-name-to-exercise mapping decisions, may also be stored. Saved mappings are specific to your account, not shared aliases for other users. Created exercises, equipment, or mappings can remain saved even if a later workout-import step fails.

Local and operational information

The app stores an active workout draft and account/session state locally to support normal operation and workout recovery. Earlier versions may have left local exercise, template, or history records. Selected photos, CSV copies, and image caches may also remain on the device.

Backend services may process operational information needed to serve requests and maintain reliability, such as IP addresses, request details, account identifiers, error codes, and timestamps. This policy does not describe their exact log contents or retention. Development builds can produce diagnostic logs; Traecer does not include a dedicated in-app behavioral analytics, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting SDK. Traecer does not collect advertising identifiers or a persistent device identifier for tracking.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information or attachments you choose to include.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, maintain your account, save and restore workouts, display History and progression, provide Ghost Sets, manage templates and custom exercises/equipment, remember preferences, process imports, maintain security and reliability, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Traecer does not sell or rent personal data, or use it for advertising.

3. Gender and body illustrations

Gender is optional. The available choices are Female, Male, and Prefer not to say. The choice selects the anatomical illustrations shown in the app. It does not determine exercise access, workout calculations, or Progress calculations.

Gender is not publicly displayed or shared with other users. Like other profile information, it is processed by our backend provider to save and load your profile. You can change it in Profile & Settings.

4. Service providers

Supabase provides authentication, database hosting, private profile-image storage, and the server-side account-deletion function. It processes account, profile, settings, and training information as needed to provide those services. See Supabase's privacy information .

Traecer uses Expo's software and EAS build services to develop and package the app. Apple provides iOS app distribution through TestFlight and the App Store. These services may process operational or distribution information under their own policies; Traecer's workout-storage backend is Supabase, not Expo/EAS. See Expo's privacy information and Apple's privacy policy .

Our privacy contact uses Gmail, so messages sent to that address are processed by Google's email service. See Google's privacy policy .

5. Photos and files

You can choose a profile image through the photo-library picker. Only the selected image is uploaded to private profile-image storage; Traecer does not upload your entire photo library. Access to uploaded avatars is restricted, and the app uses temporary signed links to display them.

The iOS app includes a photo-library permission explanation. It does not request camera, microphone, location, contacts, or advertising-tracking permission. CSV import reads a file you explicitly select through the system file picker. Selecting an image or file does not delete or modify your original photo or CSV.

6. Storage and security

Traecer uses authentication, database ownership/access policies, private avatar storage, and HTTPS connections to its backend. These controls are intended to restrict access to account-specific information. No internet-based service or device can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

Account and saved training information remain available while you keep your account, unless you remove information through supported app actions or delete the account. Archiving an exercise does not erase completed workout history.

Operational logs and backups are subject to our providers' retention arrangements. We do not promise a particular backup-erasure timeframe. Privacy/support correspondence is separate from app account records and is not automatically removed by in-app account deletion; contact us about that correspondence.

Local caches, original files, legacy records, and copies on other devices or in device backups are not all covered by the app's account-deletion cleanup.

8. Account deletion

You can request permanent deletion inside the app through Profile & Settings → Data & Privacy → Delete Account, then confirm the destructive action.

The server verifies your authenticated identity, removes files from your account's avatar-storage folder, and deletes your authentication account. Database relationships delete your profile, settings, Spotlight selections, custom exercises and equipment, templates and their exercise entries, workouts and their exercise/set records, and saved import mappings. Shared built-in exercises and equipment remain because they are not owned by your account.

Following successful deletion, the app signs you out, resets its account state, and attempts to remove your account-keyed workout draft, history/template records, and stored authentication session from the current device. This does not erase your original CSV or photos, all temporary caches or legacy local records, other-device copies, provider logs/backups, or email correspondence.

Deletion requires a working connection. If deletion cannot be confirmed, the app reports failure rather than confirming deletion; your account can remain, even if avatar cleanup has already occurred. Retry or contact us for assistance.

9. Your choices and rights

You can edit supported profile and settings fields, omit Gender or a profile photo, choose whether to import a file, and delete your account.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of your personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Send requests to traecer.privacy@gmail.com . We may need to verify your identity before releasing or changing account information. The app does not currently provide a working self-service data-export feature.

You may also complain to your local data-protection authority. In Norway, this is Datatilsynet .

10. Age eligibility

Traecer is intended for users aged 16 and older. It is not directed to children. The app does not verify your age. If you believe an underage user has provided information, contact us.

11. International processing

Service providers may process information outside Norway or your country of residence. This policy does not name the countries or transfer safeguards our providers use, because those details are not verified here. Contact traecer.privacy@gmail.com and we will share what we can confirm.

12. Changes

Updates to this policy will be reflected in its effective date. Material changes may also be explained through additional notices where appropriate.
